_Welcome to our 256th newsletter on Star Atlas! This weekly newsletter, published by Aephia Industries, focuses entirely on the development of this ambitious game. Here, we attempt to aggregate all the newsworthy tidbits revealed, primarily by the team, throughout the past week._

---

SAGE C4 Phase 3.2 entered internal testing last week, with the team hoping to release it in the coming days. Meanwhile, zXP earnings from PTR gameplay have come to an end, and we got a clearer picture of the timeline for Phase 4, with both SAGE C4 and z.ink mainnet now expected in 2027\. We wrap up with some UE5 updates and a first look at several previously unseen Fimbul ships.

_Let’s dive in!_

## SAGE C4

The Atlas Brew gave us plenty of SAGE C4 news again last week, covering the upcoming Phase 3.2 release, the end of zXP rewards for PTR gameplay, and a clearer timeline for Phase 4\. Let’s go through them one by one.

### Phase 3.2

The work planned for Phase 3.2 has been committed, and the build entered final deployment and internal DevNet testing last week. Michael (CEO) said during [Atlas Brew #230](https://youtu.be/RWyTUpIhkvw?t=821) that the team intended to test through the weekend and tentatively release the update on **Monday, November 16, or Tuesday, November 17**. A serious issue could still push that back.

Of the 159 pull requests the team had to merge into their production repo, 129 had been merged by the time the Brew was recorded, and 30 remained open. That does not mean all 30 were release blockers.

Phase 3.2 explicitly allows a ship configuration to be assigned after a fleet has been formed. Michael said this **should** let renters install configurations on already formed rented fleets, although he had not coordinated that conclusion with Anthony (the Fleet Rentals developer). If it works as described and a rental expires with the renter’s configuration still installed, the renter would lose it, much like cargo left aboard a returning rental. Michael still needed to confirm whether renters can remove a configuration before return.

One part of the new progression system did not fit into this release. The team wanted to award XP and loot in proportion to the damage each player dealt, but storing the required on-chain history would approach or exceed Solana’s 10 MB account-size limit. Michael estimated that roughly **95%** of the intended Council Rank, XP curve, tracking, and scaling work remains in Phase 3.2.

### No more zXP for playing C4 (PTR)

PTR gameplay stopped earning zXP after the daily epoch at **00:00 UTC on November 14**. The incentive began with [Phase 2](https://aephia.com/star-atlas/weekly-star-atlas-newsletter-239/), when players could link their PTR account to z.ink and earn points through C4 activity. Michael said it ran longer and paid more generously than intended. Phase 3.2 will instead concentrate testing on the game’s core systems, with Phase 4 starting to reward playtesters with ATLAS.

### Phase 4 window

The team expects Phase 3.2 testing to take roughly **30 to 60 days**. A Phase 4 release around mid-December remains possible if testing goes cleanly, but the team does not want to launch later than the week of December 14\. If the update is not ready by then, Phase 4 will be moved to January.

With that, a release of Phase 4 this year seems unlikely. The timing depends on the stability of Phase 3.2 and on which additional features the team decides to include. Phase 4 was [previously](https://aephia.com/star-atlas/weekly-star-atlas-newsletter-250/) described as a launch rehearsal and will introduce mainnet-asset snapshots and part of the existing SAGE ATLAS emissions into the PTR.

SAGE C4 and the z.ink TGE are still following a [joint-launch plan](https://aephia.com/star-atlas/weekly-star-atlas-newsletter-246/). With C4 not launching on mainnet this year, that means z.ink won’t launch this year either. Both are now expected to launch in 2027.

## UE5

Michael said last week that the Unreal Engine team was producing around 10 to 20 development patches per day while preparing a larger production package. The work includes combat-racing improvements and cooperative dogfighting.

ATMTA and Super Phoenix Sports are also in discussions to organize a [streamed UE5 event](https://x.com/SuperPhoenixSPS/status/2122057051106861465) in December. Stay tuned for a date and format.
